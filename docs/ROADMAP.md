# Product phases

The November 18 [applicant operations delivery](APPLICANT-OPERATIONS.md) implements the consultation-payment, meeting, private document, full application, opportunity/promotion, service-credit and independent application workflows. The phase descriptions below are the original roadmap, retained for context; advanced availability, automated provider submissions, banking settlement, refunds, delivery webhooks and operational retention remain future work.

## Phase 1 delivered in this repository

Public website and brand, registered consultation assessment, consent, lead storage, authentication foundation, applicant contact account, database-assigned staff roles, admin/advisor lead workspace, private notes, contact inbox, audit and durable email outbox. External service setup and configured end-to-end validation remain launch gates, not simulated features.

## Phase 2: applicant operations

Academic records and study preferences beyond the initial assessment; verified advisor assignments for ongoing cases; private document requests, quarantine, content verification and malware scanning before staff downloads; document review history; consultation types, availability, time zones and confirmed bookings. Do not enable uploads until these controls are implemented and tested.

## Phase 3: private research and application management

Scholarships and requirements in the private schema; verified sources and research history; human-reviewed matches; proposed applications with the provider and material details disclosed to the applicant; recorded authorization; preparation tasks, submission evidence, status history, provider decisions and deadline jobs. There is no public complete scholarship directory and no authoritative AI eligibility scoring.

## Phase 4: business operations

Approved admin-managed services/prices, invoices, payment claims separated from administrator verification, receipts, refunds, scoped financial reporting; delivery/bounce webhooks, preference-aware email/WhatsApp provider abstraction and secure webhook verification; scheduling and communication center. No payment collection or automatic booking is enabled by this release.

## Phase 5: operational maturity

Approved content-management workflow, monitoring, tested retention/export/deletion operations, backup restoration drills, advanced reporting, performance analysis, expanded accessibility review, Kiswahili and justified additional integrations. Production intake must not start before the applicable privacy and operational prerequisites are satisfied.
